Pizza Toast

Ingredients:

  • Toast
  • Pineapple (optional)
  • Tomato Sauce
  • LOTS OF CHEESE
  • Ham
  • or other toppings you prefer :D

Instructions:

  1. Get a piece of toast.
  2. Apply Tomato Sauce.
  3. Sprinkle lots of cheese.
  4. Place Your Chosen Toppings.
  5. Bake it in the oven or oven toaster for a few minutes.
  6. Place it on a plate and enjoy!
